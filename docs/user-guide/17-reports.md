# Reports: products, customers and reorders

Three reports under **Reports** in the menu (owner and supervisors). Every report has a period and an **Excel / CSV** button.

## Product report

**Reports → Product report** shows every product side by side:

| Column | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Shown** | Chats where the AI bot showed the product (its photo or details). |
| **In summary** | Chats where it was in the order summary the customer saw. |
| **Orders / Qty / Sales** | Orders that are not cancelled – from the bot, your team and the admin. |
| **Shown → ordered** | Of the chats that saw it, how many ordered it through the bot. A low % on a product many people see means: check its price, photos or selling points. |
| **Asked, unavailable** | Customers who wanted to order it while it was Unavailable or out of stock – sales you lost. |
| **Stock** | When you count stock for it (see **Stock and alerts** in [Products, services and delivery](#catalog)). |

Click a product to see its last 30 days, its options and **who bought it** (opens their purchases).

**What we see** writes the main findings in plain words – your best seller, products to restock, products many people see but few buy.

Two lists at the bottom:

- **Asked for while unavailable** – the product, the customer and a link to the chat. Restock, then message them.
- **Asked for, not in your catalog** – questions the bot answered with "we don't have it" (for example *"Huggies thiyenawada?"*), grouped, with how many customers asked. Things many people ask for may be worth adding to your shop.

> **Note:** Shown, In summary and the two lists count chats with the AI bot from the day this report was added. Test chats in the AI sales bot page are not counted.

## Customer purchases

**Reports → Customer purchases** lists every customer who ordered in the period: orders, total spent, average order, what they usually buy, **orders every … days** (the usual gap between their orders), last order and a link to the chat.

- Numbers on top: customers who bought, how many **bought again**, sales, average order, and the share of sales from your **top 20%** of customers.
- Filter by channel, search by name or phone, sort by most spent, most orders, latest order or name.
- Click a customer to see **every order with its items** and, per product, how many times they bought it, how often, and **when they need it again**. **Orders CSV** downloads that customer's orders.
- From the Product report, **All customers who bought it** opens this list for one product.

Cancelled orders are not counted.

## Reorder due

**Reports → Reorder due** lists customers who will probably need a product again soon – remind them before they buy somewhere else. Very useful for things people buy again and again (diapers, milk powder, cosmetics, pet food, refills).

How it decides:

- A customer who bought the product **2 or more times**: the usual gap between their purchases.
- A customer who bought it **once**: the product's **buys again every … days** (open the product on the Product report and set it at the bottom). The report tells you which bought products don't have it yet.

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Overdue** | Should have reordered already. Customers more than 60 days late are left out. |
| **Due now** | Within 3 days. |
| **Soon** | Within the window you choose (3, 7, 14 or 30 days). |

What you can do:

- **Message** copies a ready reminder (edit it if you like) – then **Chat** opens the conversation.
- Tick customers and **Save as tag** (for example *reorder-due*), then send them a **WhatsApp broadcast** with an approved template. WhatsApp only lets you send a normal message within 24 hours of the customer's last message, so for older chats use a broadcast.
