# Teach the bot – AI knowledge

**AI & automation → AI knowledge** is where you teach the bot your rules, your answers and your style. It has four tabs: **Overview**, **Knowledge**, **Documents** and **Review replies**.

## Knowledge entries

Each entry has a **customer question**, the **answer**, a **category** and a language.

| Category | How the bot uses it | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Policy** | Always known, in every chat. | Returns in 7 days, 1-year warranty, cash on delivery only in Colombo, delivery in 2–3 days. |
| **Style** | Examples of how you reply. The bot copies your tone and length. | Your best real replies. 5–10 are enough to start. |
| **General / FAQ / any other** | Looked up when a customer asks something similar. | "Do you have a shop?", "Can I pay in instalments?" |

> **Tip:** Keep policies short and clear – one rule per entry. Write style examples in the language your customers use (for example Singlish).

## Documents

Upload a **PDF, Word (.docx) or text** file (up to 10 MB) – a price list, brochure, FAQ or terms. The text becomes bot knowledge. About 20,000 characters (≈ 6 pages) are used per file, so split big documents. Reading documents is **free** (no AI credits).

## Review replies

See what the bot answered and improve it:

- Filter **All replies** or **Handed to a person**.
- **Good reply** – saved as a style example.
- **Teach a better reply** – write how it should have answered, choose *Style reply example* or *FAQ*, and **Save lesson**.

You can also rate replies directly in the Inbox with 👍 and 👎.

## What else the bot knows

- **Products and services** – names, prices, variants, photos, selling points and add-ons. See [Products, services and delivery](#catalog).
- **Delivery zones** – the fee is always calculated by the system, never by the AI.
- **AI sales bot settings** – about the business, opening hours, payment methods, tone.
- **Campaigns** – special offers and instructions for customers who came from an ad. See [Marketing](#marketing).

Changes are used by the bot right away.
