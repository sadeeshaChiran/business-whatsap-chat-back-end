# Connect WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram and Telegram

Open **Channels** in the menu. Each channel shows its status: **Connected**, **Needs attention** or **Not connected**.

## WhatsApp (official Cloud API)

{{brand}} uses the official **WhatsApp Business Cloud API** from Meta. It is fast and safe, and no phone needs to stay online.

1. Go to **Channels → WhatsApp** and press **Connect with Meta**.
2. Meta's login page opens (in the same tab, like the Facebook Page login). Log in with the Facebook account that manages your business.
3. Choose (or create) your **WhatsApp Business account** and your **phone number**.
4. Verify the number with the code Meta sends (SMS or call).
5. Finish the steps. Meta brings you back to {{brand}}, the number is connected and the **connection check** runs.
6. Test: send "hi" to your business number from another phone. It appears in the **Inbox**.

The card at the top then shows your WhatsApp business name, the number and its quality.

> **Note:** A number that is used in the normal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business **app** must be moved to the Cloud API. After that the number no longer works in the phone app – all chats are answered in {{brand}}. If you want to keep your phone app, use a new number for {{brand}}.

**Change main number**: press **Change main number** on the card. The new number REPLACES the old one; the old number keeps working until the new one is connected. Want to keep the old number too? Use **Add a number** instead.

### More than one WhatsApp number

Use different numbers for different jobs – for example **Sales**, **Customer support**, **Wholesale** or a **branch**. Every chat still arrives in one Inbox.

1. **Channels → WhatsApp → Your WhatsApp numbers → Add a number**.
2. Give it a **name** (e.g. "Wholesale") and choose what **customers use it for**.
3. Press **Continue to Meta**, choose **Add a new number** and verify it with the SMS code. You come back and the number is ready.

What happens then:

- **Replies go from the number the customer wrote to** – from the bot and from your team. A customer never gets an answer from a number they don't know.
- The **Inbox** shows a small badge with the number's name on every WhatsApp chat, and a **WhatsApp number** filter at the top of the chat list.
- Each number shows its **results for the last 30 days**: chats, new customers, orders and sales – so you see which number brings the most business.
- **Instructions for the AI bot on this number** (optional): e.g. "Wholesale customers: minimum 10 packs, give the wholesale price list". The bot also knows what the number is for.
- **AI bot answers on this number**: switch it off for a number only your team should answer (e.g. a support line). Chats on that number wait in the **Chat queue** for an agent.
- **Make main**: the main number is used for broadcasts and for chats your team starts. **Disconnect** removes an extra number; its customers get replies from the main number from then on. Chats, customers and orders stay.

Your package sets how many numbers you can connect (**Billing**). Only the owner can add or change numbers.

**Did Meta show an error?** Press **Connect with Meta** again. If it still fails, a link **Use Meta's pop-up window** appears as a second way to connect.

## Messenger

1. **Channels → Messenger → Connect Facebook Page**.
2. Log in with Facebook, allow **all** the permissions asked, and choose your Page (**Use**). Each Page shows its picture, category and linked Instagram.
3. Messages are switched on automatically. Send a message to your Page from another account to test.

## Instagram

Before you start:

- Your Instagram must be a **Professional account** (Business or Creator).
- It must be **linked to your Facebook Page** (Instagram → Settings → Accounts Center or Page settings → Linked accounts).
- In the Instagram app: **Settings → Messages and story replies → Connected tools → Allow access to messages** must be **on**.

Then: **Channels → Instagram → Connect Instagram**, log in with the Facebook account that manages the Page, and choose your **Instagram account**. Only Pages that have an Instagram Professional account linked are shown. The card then shows your @username, followers and the Facebook Page it is linked to.

> **Tip:** On Messenger and Instagram the bot always asks for a phone number before it confirms an order, so your delivery team can call the customer.

## Customer app (your own free chat app)

Every business gets **its own app** – with **your name, your logo and your colour** – at its own address, for example `…/app/vora-kids`. Customers log in with their **phone number** (a 6-digit SMS code) and add it to their **home screen**: it shows **your logo** like a real app. Messages cost nothing, and there are no limits or approvals.

**Switch it on:** **Customer app** (its own section in the menu) → turn the switch on.

**Your app** tab:

- **App name, address and colour** – the phone preview shows how the icon looks on a home screen. *Add to Home Screen* (iPhone and Android) installs **your app with your name and icon**, opening straight into it.
- **Icon:** made from the logo you upload in **Profile** (or pick another picture with *Change icon*).
- **App link and QR code** – send them to your customers.
- **Who can log in:** **Only people I add** (default) or **Anyone with the link** (new people who open your link join your list).
- **Member messages** (private chats between your customers): **Off** (default), **Same group** (people in the same group – even when the group hides its member list, each member sees the people they may message), or **By roles** – you choose exactly which roles may message which (for example *parent ↔ teacher*, *teacher ↔ teacher*; `*` = anyone).
- **Welcome message:** the first message a customer sees in your chat.

**Customers** tab – who may use your app:

- **Add people** from your contacts: **by tag** (for example *grade10*), **choose contacts**, **type numbers**, or **all my contacts**. Only numbers on this list can log in – everyone else is told they are not on your list, and no SMS is sent to them.
- See **who has the app** and **who doesn't yet**, and who turned notifications on. **Invite on WhatsApp** opens a normal WhatsApp chat from your phone with the app link (free). **Copy invite** gives you a text for WhatsApp, SMS or Facebook.
- **Roles:** give customers role tags such as *parent*, *teacher* or *vip* (**Roles** on a customer, or pick several and set roles together). Roles decide who may message whom and who may make meetings, and you can filter the list by role.
- **Online:** see who is online now or when they were last active.
- Remove someone: they can no longer open your app and they leave your groups.

**Broadcast** tab – offers, notices and reminders, **free and without limits**: write the message (with an optional photo) and send it to **everyone on the app**, to **customers with tags**, or to **members of groups**. It arrives in their app chat with a phone notification. The page shows how many have the app, how many on your list don't have it yet, and how many **read** each broadcast. Customers who reply simply continue the chat in your Inbox.

**In the Inbox:** app chats show the **App** mark. **Group chats come into the Inbox too** (shown as *<group> · group*) – your team can read and answer them there. Reply like any other chat: text, photos, files, voice messages, locations and contacts. The **AI bot** can answer too. Order messages, invoices and notes reach the app chat. Customers see **✓✓** when your team has read their message.

**Profile, News, Groups** tabs:

- **Profile:** logo, about, address, phone, open hours and website.
- **News:** post notices and offers (with a photo) and **pin** the important ones. You see how many people saw each post.
- **Groups:** communities of your customers, for example one per class. **📣 Announcements** or **💬 Everyone can chat**, **🔒 Private** or **🌐 Public**, and whether members can see the member list. Add members **by tag** or by hand (only people on your app list), make **admins**, remove people, delete any message.

**Calls** (voice and video, like WhatsApp): customers tap 📞 or 📹 in your chat – it rings on the dashboard of **everyone in your team** (*Answer* / *Ignore*), in the Agent Metra phone app and in your own app on your team's phones (as a notification with *Answer* / *Decline*, even when the app is closed – after notifications are turned on). Customers get calls the same way; a call nobody answers becomes a *Missed call* notification. Your team can also call a customer from an app chat in the Inbox (📞 / 📹 at the top). Each call is noted in the chat (*📹 Video call · 4:12* or *missed*).

**Team** tab – **you and your agents use the app as the business**: you (the owner) log into your app with the **mobile number of your Agent Metra account**; for each agent type their mobile number here. They open your app link, log in with that number (SMS code – this also confirms the number) and get the **business side** of the app: an **Inbox** of app chats and group chats (answer as the business – customers never see their number), **incoming customer calls ring their phone**, and **Meetings**. Agents see only the chats assigned to them; the owner and supervisors see everything and can plan meetings. Remove someone's access in Team → Agents and they are out of the app too.

**Meetings** tab (like Google Meet – camera, screen sharing, chat): **New meeting** → name, time, length and **who can join** (everyone, groups and/or roles). Invited people get a notification and see it on their Chats page; the meeting opens 15 minutes before the start. **Copy link** or **Share to groups** posts a Join link in group chats – **only invited people can join**, even if the link is passed on. **Who else can make meetings:** pick roles (for example *teacher*) – those customers can make meetings for their own groups and the people they may message.

**Reports** tab: when a customer reports a member or a message, it appears here with a copy of the message, who reported it and who it is about. **Mark handled** or **Remove from app**.

**What customers see** – a bottom menu like a real app:

- **Chats** – your business chat **pinned at the top** (with your broadcasts), then their private chats with other members (when you allow it) and the meetings they are invited to. **New message** starts a chat with someone they may message.
- **News** and **Groups**.
- **Me** – photo, name, their **member ID** (for example *#K7Q2M*), *show my name in groups*, *members can message me*, **notifications**, *Add to Home Screen*, light / dark, log out, delete account.

**Ticks and online:** ✓ sent, ✓✓ delivered, **blue ✓✓** read. In groups, your own messages show how many members **got** and **read** them. Chats show **online** / *last seen* – customers can hide it under **Me → Show when I'm online** (then they don't see others' either).

**Security of member chats (end-to-end encrypted, like WhatsApp):** private messages, photos and files between members are locked on the phone and can be opened **only on the two people's phones** – not by your business and not by us. Calls between members are encrypted the same way. A **security code** in the chat lets two people check they are really talking to each other. If someone reports a message, the report carries the text **as the reporter shared it**. Logging out on a phone removes its key, so old member messages can't be read there any more. Your own chat with customers, group chats and meetings are protected in transit but your team can read them (so you can answer in the Inbox).

**Privacy:** customers never see each other's phone numbers. Other members see a name (if the customer allows it) or the **member ID**. Anyone can **block** or **report** another member. The app has its own login – it can never open your business account.

**Phone notifications:** customers tap **Turn on** in the app. New replies, broadcasts, news, group messages, member messages (shown as *🔒 New message*), calls and meetings then show on the phone, even when the app is closed. Muted groups and businesses send nothing. On iPhone this works after *Share → Add to Home Screen* (iOS 16.4 or newer).

> The older shared app at `/chat` (all businesses in one list) still works for the same customers.

## Telegram

Connect your **own Telegram bot**. It is **free** and needs no approval or business documents:

- Customers who press **Start** on your bot reach the Inbox. You, your team and the AI bot reply. There is **no 24-hour window**.
- **Announcements** go to your Telegram **channel** and / or to every follower's chat, **free**, with no daily limit.

**Connect (2 minutes)**

1. In Telegram, open **@BotFather** (the official one with the blue tick) and send **/newbot**.
2. Give it a name (for example *VORA Kids*) and a username that ends in **bot** (for example *vorakids_bot*).
3. Copy the **token** BotFather sends (it looks like `123456789:AAH…`).
4. **Channels → Telegram**: paste the token → **Connect**.

The page then shows your bot, the number of followers, a **start link** and a **QR code**. Put the link or QR on WhatsApp, Facebook, posters or the class board. Customers press **Start** once and they are in.

> **Good to know:** Telegram does not let a bot message a phone number first. A customer must press **Start** on your bot once. After that you can message them any time, for free.

**Welcome message:** sent automatically when someone presses Start. Use `{name}` and `{business}`.

**Telegram channel (notice board):** create a channel in Telegram, make your bot an **admin** with *Post messages*, then type the channel's @username on the page and press **Link channel**. It often links by itself once the bot is admin.

**Announcements:** write the message (optionally with a photo) and choose **Channel**, **Followers' chats** or both. To reach only some customers, add **tags** (for example *grade10*): tag customers in CRM first. `{name}` puts each follower's first name in their own chat. The page shows how many received it and how many failed (for example people who blocked the bot). You can **Stop** an announcement while it is sending.

**Groups & channels only for your customers:** make a private Telegram **group** or **channel** (for example *Grade 10 – 2026*), add your bot as an **admin** with *Invite users via link* and *Ban users* (channels: also *Post messages*). It appears under **Groups & channels for your customers** by itself.

- **Invite** followers by **tag**, by hand or all of them: each gets a private message from your bot with a **Join** button.
- The join link only sends a **join request**: the bot lets in the people you invited and **declines everyone else**, so a forwarded link is useless.
- *Also let in followers with these tags* – people you tag later get in without a new invite.
- **People:** who joined, who is invited, who left, who asked without an invite. **Remove** someone and they are taken out (and can't come back with the link).
- **Post** into the group or channel from here.
- Telegram does not let bots create groups – you make it in Telegram, and the bot manages who gets in.

You can send text, photos (up to 10 MB), files, videos and audio (up to 50 MB), locations and contacts. Voice notes recorded in the app can't be sent to Telegram – send an audio file instead.

## TikTok

> **Hidden for now.** This section is for when TikTok approves the app. Until then the TikTok menu, page and package switch are hidden (web app `VITE_TIKTOK_ENABLED=false`, API without `TIKTOK_APP_ID` / `TIKTOK_APP_SECRET`). The in-app guide does not show this section yet.

Customers who send a **direct message to your TikTok Business account** reach the same Inbox, and the AI bot can answer them like any other chat.

What you need:

- A **TikTok Business account** (TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Account → *Switch to Business Account*).
- TikTok business messaging is **not available in the EU (EEA), the UK and Switzerland**.

Then: **Channels → TikTok → Connect TikTok**, log in on TikTok's own page and **allow every permission** it asks for. The page then shows your @username, how many TikTok customers wrote and when the last message arrived. **Check connection** tests the login, the account and the webhook.

TikTok has its own rules. They apply **only to TikTok chats** – WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram work exactly as before:

- You can reply for **48 hours** after the customer's last message.
- You can send at most **10 messages in a row**. When the customer replies, you can send 10 more. A bar above the message box shows the hours and messages left.
- You can send **text and photos** (JPG or PNG, up to 3 MB). Files and voice notes can't be sent – invoices go as a link.
- Only the customer can start a chat.

In the Inbox, TikTok chats have the TikTok mark, and a **TikTok** filter button appears as soon as you have a TikTok chat.

> **Tip:** For the AI bot a TikTok chat is like an Instagram chat – same answers, same order steps (it asks for a phone number before it confirms an order).

## SMS

Send **SMS** with **your own Notify.lk account** – your sender name (for example *VORAKIDS*), your SMS credit. Good for offers, reminders and notices to customers who are not on WhatsApp or your app, and for one-off messages from a chat.

**Connect (2 minutes):** make an account at **app.notify.lk** and add credit. Ask Notify.lk for your **sender name** (until it is approved, *NotifyDEMO* only sends to your own test numbers). Copy the **User ID** and **API key** (Settings → API Keys), then **Channels → SMS** → paste them with your sender name → **Check and connect**. The page shows your **Notify.lk balance**, SMS sent in the last 30 days, and has a **test SMS** button. The API key is never shown again after saving.

**Line added to every SMS** (optional): for example *Reply on WhatsApp 077 123 4567*. SMS from a sender name **can't be answered**, so tell customers where to reply.

**SMS campaigns** (**Marketing → SMS campaigns**): write the message (`{name}` = the customer's first name), choose **contacts with tags**, **all contacts with a number**, or **type / paste numbers** (one per line, optionally `0771234567, Nimal`). The page counts the numbers and the SMS: **160 characters per SMS in English, 70 in Sinhala / Tamil / emoji** (longer messages are sent as 2, 3 … SMS). A phone preview shows exactly what arrives. While sending you see sent / failed / waiting, and you can **Stop**. If Notify.lk says there is no credit, the campaign stops by itself.

**From a chat:** in the Inbox, open a chat → **⋯ → Send SMS** – it goes to the customer's mobile number (WhatsApp number, app number, or the number saved in the contact). Agents can do this for their own chats.

**In the chat:** every SMS you send (campaign or one-off) also shows in that customer's chat with the label *Sent by SMS*, so the team sees it. It is not shown inside the customer app.

**Do-not-SMS list:** numbers that must never get SMS from you (Channels → SMS). Campaigns skip them, and so are WhatsApp customers who said STOP.

## The connection check

Every channel page has a live **connection check**. It asks Meta what is working right now and shows each item in plain words:

- Access token works
- Number quality / number health
- Number is registered for the Cloud API
- Messages are sent to {{brand}}
- Meta is calling {{brand}} (the last message was accepted)

When something is red, press **Fix it** if the button is shown (for example *register the number* – you need your 6-digit WhatsApp two-step PIN), or follow the text. Press **Check again** after a fix. If it still shows red, send a screenshot of the check to our support team.

## Package and channels

## Meta's 24-hour rule

On WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram you can reply freely for **24 hours after the customer's last message**. After that:

- **WhatsApp**: only an approved **template** can be sent (see [Marketing](#marketing)).
- **Messenger / Instagram**: Meta may refuse normal messages. Wait for the customer to write again.
