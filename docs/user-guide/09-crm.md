# CRM – contacts, pipeline and tasks

The CRM keeps every customer from WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram in one list, shows where each one is in your sales process, and reminds your team what to do next.

## Stages

Every customer has a **stage**:

**New** → **Contacted** → **Qualified** → **Proposal** → **Won** / **Lost**

The bot moves customers forward automatically: *Contacted* when it replies, *Qualified* when the customer is interested, *Proposal* while a booking is being made, *Won* when they order or a booking is confirmed, *Lost* when they cancel with a reason. It never moves a customer back. Won and Lost stay until you change them – or until the customer clicks one of your ads again (a new ad click is a new deal, so they start again at New). Flows and forms can also set the stage. Every stage change is kept with its date, so results can be counted per visit.

## Customer insights

**CRM → Customer insights** looks at the history of every customer on WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram and shows who buys again, who slipped away and who keeps saying no.

### Visits

Every time a customer comes to you is a **visit**:

- their first message,
- a click on one of your ads, or
- a message after **3 or more quiet days**.

Each visit gets its own result: **Ordered**, **Booked**, **Won**, **Lost**, **Interested**, **Talking** or **No answer**. A customer who bought in March, came back in May and did not buy has two visits: one bought, one not.

### The numbers on top

Customers, how many **bought** (and % of customers), **bought again** (2 or more visits with a purchase), **lost, never bought**, **in talks now** (last message in the last 7 days, no result yet) and **sales**. Choose the period (30 days to all time) and the channel (All, WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram).

**What we see** writes the main findings in plain words, for example "5 customers bought before but did not buy on their last visit". **By channel** compares WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram: customers, buyers, visits won / lost / open and sales.

### Customer groups

| Group | Who | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **In talks now** | Last visit still open, last message in the last 7 days | Answer quickly and follow up today. |
| **Loyal buyers** | Bought in 2+ visits and again last time | Thank them, VIP offer, ask for a review. |
| **Repeat buyers – no order last time** | Bought in 2+ visits, last visit without an order | Ask what went wrong, personal come-back offer. |
| **Bought once (last visit)** | One purchase, in the latest visit | Follow up after delivery, suggest a second product. |
| **Bought before – not this time** | One earlier purchase, came back but did not buy | Find out why, come-back offer. |
| **Lost again and again** | Lost in 2+ visits, never bought | Read their chats for the common reason. |
| **Lost once** | Lost once, never bought | A friendly offer later. |
| **Came many times, never bought** | 2+ visits, no order, no clear "no" | Ask what stops them, first-order offer. |
| **Went quiet** | One visit, no order, quiet for 7+ days | One follow-up, then let them be. |

Each group shows how many customers it has, the split per channel and their sales. **Show customers** lists them; **Save as tag** puts a tag (for example *win-back*) on all of them – then use the tag in **WhatsApp broadcasts**, **Audiences** or the **Contacts** filter.

### One customer's history

In the list, each customer has a row of dots – one per visit, oldest to newest: **coloured = bought**, **red = lost**, **grey = no result**, a ring = came from an ad. Click the customer to see every visit with its date, where it came from (first message, came back, or the ad's name) and the result, plus **Open chat**.

## CRM overview

Numbers: contacts, buying customers, sales in 30 days, open pipeline value, active in 7 days, overdue tasks. Charts: pipeline by stage, new contacts per day, channels, top tags, best customers and follow-ups. Buttons **New contact** and **New task**.

## Contacts

- Search by name, phone or email. Filter by stage, channel, tag and owner.
- Sort by recent activity, newest, most spent, biggest deal, most orders or name.
- Columns: contact, stage, tags, owner, deal value, orders, spent, last activity.
- Select contacts to **add/remove a tag**, **Move to stage…** or **Set owner…** (up to 500 at once).
- Click a contact to open the **contact drawer**: open the chat, orders, spent, bookings, messages, stage, deal value, owner, phone, email, tags, tasks, notes and a timeline.

### Import and export

- **Import** a CSV file with a `phone` column (also accepted: whatsapp, mobile, number) and optional `name`, `email`, `tags` (separate tags with `;` or `|`). Numbers like 07x… become 947x…. Existing contacts are updated, new tags are added.
- **Export** downloads all contacts as CSV (name, channel, phone, email, tags, stage, orders, total spent, deal value, last activity).

### New contact

Name, WhatsApp number, email, stage, deal value, tags and a first note.

## Pipeline

A board with one column per stage. **Drag a customer** to the next stage. Each column shows the number of customers and the deal value. Top: open pipeline, won and win rate.

## Tasks

- **New task**: what needs to be done, type (Follow-up, Call, WhatsApp message, Meeting / visit, Payment, Delivery, Other), priority (High, Normal, Low), customer, who does it, notes and due date.
- Lists: **My tasks**, **Made by me**, **Everyone**; Open / Done.
- Open tasks are grouped: Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, Next 7 days, Later, No due date.
- You get a notification **one hour before** a task is due.

## Customer notes

**CRM → Customer notes** shows all notes about customers – written by you, your agents or the bot (for example "🙋 Customer needs a person"). Notes are never shown to customers.

## Labels and tags

- **Tags** group customers (for example *wholesale*, *VIP*, *Kandy*). Use them in filters, broadcasts and audiences.
- **Labels** (CRM → Labels) are organisation labels you create with a name and colour and put on chats from the chat header.
