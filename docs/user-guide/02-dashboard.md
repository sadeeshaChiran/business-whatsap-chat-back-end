# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first page after login. It shows how your business is doing and what needs your attention now.

## Getting around

Use the **quick dock** at the bottom of the screen (on phones: the bottom bar and its **+** button) to jump to Inbox, Orders, Products, Reports or Settings. The **WhatsApp**, **AI bot** and **Package** chips are links too, and most cards have a link in their top-right corner (Pipeline, Bot reports, Agent performance, Channels).

## Period

Choose **Today**, **7 days** or **30 days** at the top. All numbers follow this choice.

## What the numbers say

A row of short findings written from your numbers – for example *"24 chats – up 20% vs the previous period"*, *"Busiest at 7pm"*, *"WhatsApp leads – 80% of messages"*, *"29% of chats buy"*, your **top seller**, and warnings such as many failed messages or slow team replies. Click a finding to open the matching page.

## Headline numbers

| Card | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversations** | Chats with messages in the period. |
| **New contacts** | Customers who wrote to you for the first time. |
| **Automated replies %** | How many replies were sent without a person: AI, follow-ups, campaigns – compared with your team's replies. |
| **Orders** (or **Bookings**) | Orders or bookings made in the period and their value. |

## Needs attention

This card lists things you should act on:

- **Unread chats** – customers waiting for someone to read them.
- **Customers waiting for a reply** – the last message is from the customer.
- **In the chat queue** – chats that no agent has taken yet.
- **Pending orders** – orders to confirm.
- **Bookings to confirm**.
- **Failed messages (7 days)** – messages Meta did not deliver. Open them to see why.

Click any line to open the matching list.

## Other cards

- **Sales trend** – revenue (area) and orders (line) per day, with the previous period's revenue for comparison.
- **Who answered customers** – a ring showing replies by the AI bot, follow-ups, your team and campaigns, plus how fast the bot and the team answer.
- **Customer journey** – chats → customers → orders → delivered (or bookings → done), with how many go on to each next step.
- **Order performance** and **Bookings** – status of orders and bookings.
- **Lead pipeline** – how many chats are New, Contacted, Qualified, Offer sent, Won or Lost.
- **Message activity** – messages in and out per day.
- **Credits** – how many AI credits you used this month and how many are left. See [Billing and credits](#billing).
- **Message delivery** – sent, delivered, read and failed.
- **Channels** – a ring with each channel's share of messages, customers and how many chats were answered.
- **Busiest hours** – a 24-hour heat strip of when customers write (the busiest hour is ringed, night hours are marked). Use it to plan agent shifts.
- **Best sellers** – your top products by order value (or, before the first sale, the products the bot shows most).
- **Recent conversations** – jump straight into the latest chats.

## Getting started card

Until your setup is complete, a **Getting started** card shows the steps that are left (package, channel, catalog, delivery fees, bot details, policies and style, switch the bot on) with a button for each.

## Agent dashboard

Agents see their own dashboard: WhatsApp connection and AI bot status, **Waiting for you** (chats to accept), **Active chats**, **Unread messages** and their chat list.
