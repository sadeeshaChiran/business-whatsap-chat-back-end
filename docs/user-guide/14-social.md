# Social – comments and posts

The **Social** menu manages your Facebook Page and Instagram: answer comments (with AI), create and schedule posts, and see how your posts perform. It needs your Page connected (see [Channels](#channels)).

## Comments

All comments from Facebook and Instagram in one list.

- Filters: **To answer**, **Replied**, **Hidden**, **Done**, **All**, and by platform. **Load comments from Meta** fetches the latest.
- For each comment: **Reply publicly**, **Send privately** (a private message to the person), **Hide / Unhide**, **Delete**, or **Done, no reply**.
- **AI on new comments**: *Off*, *Suggest a reply* (you approve it) or *Reply automatically*. Options: **also send a private message** and **hide spam**.
- Badges: "AI suggestion ready", "AI replied", and "The AI thinks this is spam – you can hide it."

> **Tip:** To send a fixed reply or a menu to everyone who comments a keyword (for example "PRICE"), use an automation flow with the **Comment on a post** trigger. See [Automation](#automation).

## Create post

Choose **what to share**, then a few short steps with a live phone preview on the right:

| Format | What it is | Media |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Post** | Photos, one video or text in the feed | Up to **10** photos (JPG / PNG), **or** one video (MP4 / MOV). Instagram needs a photo or a video. |
| **Reel** | One vertical video that reaches new people | One video, vertical 9:16 looks best. Facebook reels: **3–90 seconds**; Instagram: up to 15 minutes. |
| **Story** | One photo or video for 24 hours | One photo or video (videos up to **60 seconds**). Stories have no caption – put the text on the photo or video. |

1. **Share to** – tap your Facebook Page, your Instagram account or both.
2. **Photos / video** – drop files on the box, click to choose, or paste a photo into the caption. Uploads show their progress. Drag photos to change the order – the first photo is the **cover**; several photos become a swipeable carousel on Instagram. A video in a normal post is shown as a reel on Instagram.
3. **Caption** (posts and reels) – type, add emojis with one tap, add a saved **hashtag set**, or **Write with AI** (topic, language: English, Sinhala, Singlish or Tamil, and tone). Instagram allows 2,200 characters and 30 hashtags.
4. **Link** (posts only) – optional (links are not clickable in Instagram captions).
5. **When** – **Now**, **Schedule** (quick choices like *Today 7 pm*, *Tomorrow 9 am*, *Saturday 10 am*) or **Save draft**.

**Videos are published in the background.** Meta needs a few minutes to process a video, so after you press *Share now* you can leave the page – you get a notification (*"Your reel is live"*) when it is published, or a message saying what went wrong. In **Drafts & scheduled** the post shows *publishing…* until it is done.

The preview shows it the way the apps do – Facebook's photo grid (1, 2, 3 or 4+ photos) or video, Instagram's carousel, a full-screen reel with the caption over the video, and a story with its progress bar. **Before you share** lists anything to fix (for example a landscape video for a reel, or a Facebook reel longer than 90 seconds). Drafts and scheduled posts are listed under the composer – press ✎ to change one or 🗑 to cancel it.

> Videos up to 200 MB by default. If uploads of big videos fail, ask your server admin to raise the upload limit (see the deployment notes).

## Content calendar

A month view of scheduled and published posts. Press **+** on a day to plan a post. **Drafts without a date** are listed on the side.

## Published posts

Everything on your Page and Instagram – **All · Posts · Videos · Reels · Stories** (stories only while they are live, 24 hours). Totals for posts, likes, comments, shares and engagement per post, and the **★ Top post**. Sort by newest, most engagement, most likes or most comments, and search the text.

**See how one post reached people:** click a post (or **Results**). It opens on its own – the photo, video or reel plays – with:

- **Reached** (different people who saw it), **Views / plays** and the **engagement rate** (likes, comments, shares and saves ÷ people reached), with a short verdict.
- Likes and comments compared with your **average** post.
- **Where the reach came from** – organic, paid (ads) and through shares (Facebook).
- **Reactions** by type (👍 ❤️ 😮 …), and for videos and reels **average watch time**, replays, complete views and a **still watching** curve.
- Saves, profile visits, new followers, clicks and story replies where Meta shares them.

Use **←** / **→** (or the arrows) to go through your posts one by one, **Esc** to close. Meta needs a few hours after posting before reach numbers appear. All numbers come from the Page connection you already have – no new Meta permission is needed.

**Open** (on Facebook / Instagram), **Post again** (copies the text into a new post), **Edit** (Facebook posts only – Instagram captions can't be edited by apps) and **Delete**. Instagram may refuse deleting some posts – then delete them in the Instagram app.

## Social analytics

Last 7, 28 or 90 days:

- Facebook: people reached, Page views, post engagement, new followers.
- Instagram: accounts reached, accounts engaged, interactions, profile views.
- Reach per day, top posts, and the **best time to post** (Sri Lanka time).

## Saved replies

Ready answers for comments and messages: **public replies**, **private messages** and **hashtag sets**. Use `{name}` for the person's name. **Add example replies** gives you a starting set.

## Page & Instagram

Shows the connected Facebook Page and Instagram account. **Switch Page** or **Reconnect Page** here. If it says "No Instagram account linked", link your Instagram Professional account to the Page and reconnect.
