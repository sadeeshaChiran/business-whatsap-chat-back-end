# Social – comments and posts

The **Social** menu manages your Facebook Page and Instagram: answer comments (with AI), create and schedule posts, and see how your posts perform. It needs your Page connected (see [Channels](#channels)).

## Comments

All comments from Facebook and Instagram in one list.

- Filters: **To answer**, **Replied**, **Hidden**, **Done**, **All**, and by platform. **Load comments from Meta** fetches the latest.
- For each comment: **Reply publicly**, **Send privately** (a private message to the person), **Hide / Unhide**, **Delete**, or **Done, no reply**.
- **AI on new comments**: *Off*, *Suggest a reply* (you approve it) or *Reply automatically*. Options: **also send a private message** and **hide spam**.
- Badges: "AI suggestion ready", "AI replied", and "The AI thinks this is spam – you can hide it."

> **Tip:** To send a fixed reply or a menu to everyone who comments a keyword (for example "PRICE"), use an automation flow with the **Comment on a post** trigger. See [Automation](#automation).

## Create post

Five short steps, with a live phone preview on the right:

1. **Post to** – tap your Facebook Page, your Instagram account or both.
2. **Photos** – drop photos on the box, click to choose, or paste one into the caption. Up to **10** (JPG or PNG). Drag to change the order – the first photo is the **cover**. Instagram needs at least one photo; several photos become a swipeable carousel.
3. **Caption** – type, add emojis with one tap, add a saved **hashtag set**, or **Write with AI** (topic, language: English, Sinhala, Singlish or Tamil, and tone). Instagram allows 2,200 characters and 30 hashtags.
4. **Link** – optional (links are not clickable in Instagram captions).
5. **When** – **Post now**, **Schedule** (quick choices like *Today 7 pm*, *Tomorrow 9 am*, *Saturday 10 am*) or **Save draft**.

The preview shows the post the way it looks in the feed – Facebook's photo grid (1, 2, 3 or 4+ photos) and Instagram's carousel, *… more* after the first 125 characters, and *See more* on Facebook. **Before you post** lists anything to fix. Drafts and scheduled posts are listed under the composer – press ✎ to change one or 🗑 to cancel it.

## Content calendar

A month view of scheduled and published posts. Press **+** on a day to plan a post. **Drafts without a date** are listed on the side.

## Published posts

Your recent posts with their results: totals for posts, likes, comments, shares and engagement per post, and the **★ Top post**. Sort by newest, most engagement, most likes or most comments, and search the text. **Open**, **Post again** (copies the text into a new post), **Edit** (Facebook only – Instagram captions can't be edited by apps) and **Delete**. Instagram may refuse deleting some posts – then delete them in the Instagram app.

## Social analytics

Last 7, 28 or 90 days:

- Facebook: people reached, Page views, post engagement, new followers.
- Instagram: accounts reached, accounts engaged, interactions, profile views.
- Reach per day, top posts, and the **best time to post** (Sri Lanka time).

## Saved replies

Ready answers for comments and messages: **public replies**, **private messages** and **hashtag sets**. Use `{name}` for the person's name. **Add example replies** gives you a starting set.

## Page & Instagram

Shows the connected Facebook Page and Instagram account. **Switch Page** or **Reconnect Page** here. If it says "No Instagram account linked", link your Instagram Professional account to the Page and reconnect.
