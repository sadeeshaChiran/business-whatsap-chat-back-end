# The AI sales bot

The AI sales bot answers your customers day and night in **Sinhala, Singlish, Tamil or English**. It knows your products, services, prices and delivery fees, takes orders and bookings, and hands the chat to a person when needed.

Open **AI & automation → AI sales bot**.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Bot replies to customers** | The main switch. Off = every chat waits for a person. |
| **Bot name (optional)** | Empty = the bot replies as your business. |
| **Reply language** | **Same as the customer** (recommended), or always Sinhala / Singlish / Tamil / English. |
| **Products / services** | Follows Settings → Business type. |
| **Send invoice PDF automatically** | On: after the bot saves or changes an order, the customer gets an invoice PDF. |
| **Switch the bot off when a person is needed** | Off (default): the bot keeps answering simple questions while your team takes over. On: the bot goes quiet for that customer. |
| **Follow up interested customers** | Sends a friendly reminder to customers who were interested but went quiet (see below). |
| **Tone** | For example "friendly, short, helpful". |
| **Greeting** | Your first message. Empty = an automatic warm welcome. |
| **About the business** | What you do, where you are, what makes you special. Write it like you would brief a new staff member. |
| **Opening hours** | Also used for booking times. |
| **Payment methods** | Cash on delivery, bank transfer, card… |

Press **Save settings**. The side panel **What the bot knows** links to Products / Services, Delivery zones and AI knowledge.

## Test chat

Open the **Test chat** tab and write like a customer. Under each reply you see the language, how fast it was, the credits used and what the bot decided (for example "order saved"). **Nothing is saved or sent to anyone**. Press **New chat** to start again. Test before you switch the bot on.

## What the bot does

- **Answers questions** from your catalog, policies, FAQs and documents.
- **Sells only what you have**: products with "Show to the sales bot" on, at your real prices. It never invents products, prices or discounts.
- **Takes orders**: it shows a summary with the real prices and delivery fee, and saves the order **only after the customer agrees**. On Messenger and Instagram it always asks for a phone number first.
- **Changes and cancellations**: a **Pending** order can be changed or cancelled by the customer in chat. Confirmed or Processing orders only create a request – your team decides. Shipped orders go to a person.
- **Bookings** (services): checks your opening hours and free times; if a time is taken it offers the 3 nearest free times.
- **Leads**: for real estate, finance, custom work and similar, it collects the need, budget, location and a good time to call.
- **Ads**: when a customer comes from your ad, it starts with what the ad offers.
- **Notes**: special requests ("deliver quickly") go into the order note; other details into the customer notes.

## When the bot hands over to a person

The bot gives the chat to your team when:

- the customer asks for a person or a call,
- complaints, refunds, damaged or late orders, payment problems or bank slips,
- price negotiation, bulk or custom prices,
- it can't find the answer, or the customer seems angry or confused,
- the same question comes 3 times.

You get a notification, a client note "🙋 Customer needs a person – reason", and the chat goes to an online agent (or the Chat queue).

## Follow-ups

Customers who showed interest and then went quiet get a short, friendly follow-up:

- **1st follow-up after**: 1, 2, 3 (recommended), 4 or 6 hours.
- **2nd follow-up after**: none, 18, 20 or 22 hours (recommended).
- At most 2 per customer, only between **9:00 and 20:30** (Sri Lanka time), and always inside WhatsApp's 24-hour window.
- Follow-ups stop when the customer orders, says no, a person takes over or the AI reply is off.

The settings show the last 30 days: how many were sent and how many customers ordered after a follow-up. You can stop or resume the follow-up for one customer in the chat's contact panel.

## Bot per customer

**AI & automation → Bot per customer** lists every customer with platform, language, **Manual** (bot off for them) and last seen. Use **Disable bot** / **Enable bot** to switch the bot for one customer – for example a wholesale buyer you always answer yourself.

## Bot reports

**AI & automation → Bot reports** (last 7, 30 or 90 days – history depends on your package):

- **Sales**: chats that became a sale, new chats, orders, order value, bookings, leads, chats handed to a person.
- **Chats and orders per day** chart.
- **What the bot did**: orders taken, changes and cancellations, bookings, invoices sent.
- **Follow-ups**: sent and ordered (within 7 days).
- Your 👍/👎 ratings, why chats went to a person, languages, replies per channel, best-selling products, when the bot works, reply speed and credits per reply.

## Credits

The bot uses AI **credits**: 1 credit ≈ 1 whole customer chat (about 8 AI replies), so one reply is about 0.1–0.2 credit. Replies by your team are free. See [Billing and credits](#billing).
