# Inbox – all your chats in one place

The **Inbox** shows every chat from WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram. The owner sees all chats; agents see **My chats** (only their customers).

## The chat list

Each row shows:

- The customer's name (or number) with a small **channel badge** (WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram).
- The time of the last message (today's time, "Yesterday" or a date) and a preview with ticks (sent, delivered, read).
- **📣 Ad** or **shared post** chip when the customer came from one of your ads or posts.
- **Interested** / **Ready to buy** when the bot thinks the customer may buy (follow-up).
- The agent who has the chat (👤 name) or **To accept** for agents.
- **Bot** (AI is answering) or **Manual** (a person is answering).
- Unread count and up to 3 tags.

The browser tab shows the number of unread chats, for example "(3)".

## Filters and search

- Channel: **All**, **WhatsApp**, **Messenger**, **Instagram**.
- Chips: **All**, **Unread**, **Assigned** (agents: **To accept**), **From ads**, **Starred**, **Follow-up**.
- **Tags**: click one or more tags to see only those customers. **Clear** removes the filter.
- Search box: "Search or start a new chat" – by name or phone number.
- Right-click a chat (or long-press on a phone, or the ⌄ button) for **Star chat** and **Mark as unread**. Stars are saved in this browser.

## Inside a chat

The header has:

- **AI reply** switch – on: the bot answers this customer. Off: only people answer (Manual).
- **Orders (N)** and **Notes (N)** for this customer.
- **Create order** (or **Add booking** for services).
- **Show contact info** – the side panel.
- Agents: **Hand back to the team**.

Under the header is the **tag bar** – click a tag to add or remove it.

## Chats that came from your ads

When a customer writes to you from a Facebook or Instagram ad (Click-to-WhatsApp, Click-to-Messenger) or from a shared post, {{brand}} shows it clearly – just like WhatsApp does:

- **Ad card on the message**: the message that came from the ad has a small card above it with the ad's photo, **From your ad** (or **From your shared post**), the ad title, the text and the link.
- **Banner at the top of the chat**: "**Last came from your WhatsApp ad**" with the newest ad the customer clicked.
- **Customers who come back from other ads**: if the same customer clicks two or more of your ads (for example first "Websites from Rs 20,000", a week later "Logo design Rs 5,000"), the banner says "**came from 2 different ads**" and lists the **earlier ads** under the latest one. Every message still shows its own ad card, so you can see which ad started which question.
- The list shows the **📣 Ad** chip, and the **From ads** filter shows only ad chats.

**Messenger and Instagram** work the same way. Two extra cases are shown too:

- **The customer tapped your ad or the post's Send message button**: when Meta tells us ("ad clicks" on), the chat appears **right away** – before the customer types – with the ad card and your greeting, marked **📣 Ad**, and you get a **New lead from your ad** notification. Say hello quickly!
- **Ads with a welcome message**: Messenger shows the customer your ad card and greeting ("Hi Sadeesha! Please let us know how we can help you.") but Meta often does not send them to us. When the customer writes (even just "Hi"), {{brand}} reads the last messages of that chat from Meta and puts the **ad card and the greeting into the chat**, exactly as the customer sees them. The newest card marks the chat as coming from that ad / post.
- The customer **replied to your ad or post card** ("You replied to …" in their app): the card is shown above their reply, and the reply quotes it.
- On Instagram, a **reply to your story** shows the story picture ("📖 Your story") above the customer's message.

The AI bot also sees the ad. It answers about what the ad offers first (price, offer, details), so the customer doesn't have to explain.

To see results per ad (who ordered, who booked, who was lost), open **Marketing → Ads & leads**. See [Ads and leads](#ads).

## Taking over from the bot

- Turn **AI reply** off to answer yourself. The bot stays quiet for this customer until you turn it on again.
- When an agent **accepts** a chat, the bot keeps helping. The agent can switch AI reply off in the chat header to reply alone.
- When the bot needs a person (complaint, refund, price discount, the customer asks for a person…), it writes a **client note** "🙋 Customer needs a person", sends you a notification and gives the chat to an agent. See [AI sales bot](#ai-sales-bot).

## Writing messages

- Type and press Enter (Shift+Enter for a new line).
- **Reply to a message** (quote): hover a message, open its ⌄ menu and choose **Reply**. On WhatsApp the customer sees it as a real reply.
- **Attach** (📎): Document, Photos & videos, Camera, Audio, and on WhatsApp also Location, Contact and stickers. Hold the microphone for a **voice note**. Files up to 16 MB; big photos are made smaller automatically.
- Drag files into the chat to send them.
- A message that failed shows a red mark – **retry** or **delete** it.
- **Internal note**: switch the composer to "internal note". The note is yellow and the customer never sees it.
- **Templates** (Messenger / Instagram): send a card with a title, text, image and buttons.

When WhatsApp's 24-hour window is closed you see "Send a template to restart the chat" – see [Marketing](#marketing).

## Contact info panel

- Name (editable), **Copy number**, status, channel.
- Quick actions: AI reply on/off, Add note, New order.
- **Follow-up**: what the bot thinks (Ready to buy, Interested, Browsing, Not buying now), the next follow-up, **Stop** or **Resume**.
- **Assigned to**: the owner or a supervisor can give the chat to any agent.
- **Pipeline stage**: *New, Contacted, Qualified, Offer sent, Won, Lost*. The owner, a supervisor and the agent of the customer can pick it. The bot keeps moving the stage forward by itself (marked **auto**); a stage picked by a person is marked **set by hand**, and the bot never changes a **Won** or **Lost** set by hand.
- **Tags**: add tags, create new tags (owner) with 8 colours.
- **Notes**: the last notes; **View all**. Quick notes like "Prefers cash on delivery" or "Call before delivery".

## Rating the bot's replies

Under each bot reply: 👍 (good) or 👎 (wrong). After 👎 you can type the **better reply** – the bot learns it. See [AI knowledge](#ai-knowledge).

## Creating an order or booking from a chat

Press **Create order**: choose products from your catalog, customer name, phone, delivery address and area, payment (Cash on delivery, Bank transfer, Online payment) and a note. The subtotal, delivery fee and total are calculated for you. For services, **Add booking**: service, date, time, name and note.

## Chat queue (owner)

**Chat queue** lists chats waiting for an agent – for example when no agent is online. They are given out automatically when someone goes online, or choose **Assign to** an agent yourself. The side list shows which agents are online.

## For agents: My chats

- New chats are **accepted automatically** (owner setting, on by default). When the owner turned it off, a new chat shows "**New chat for you**" in a bar just above the message box: press **Accept** or **Pass on**. Nothing is sent to the customer.
- **Pass on** and **Hand back to the team** give the chat to another online agent, or back to the queue.
- Switch yourself **Online** in the top bar to get new chats.
