# Orders and bookings

## Orders

**Sales → Orders** lists every order – made by the bot, by your team from a chat, or added by hand.

**Statuses**: **Pending** → **Confirmed** → **Processing** → **Shipped** → **Delivered**, or **Cancelled**.

- Tabs: All orders, Pending, Processing, Delivered. Filter by agent.
- Open an order to change items, address, status or add an **Admin note**, then **Save Changes**.
- **Agents** see the orders of their own customers and can change the status, edit, send the invoice or delete them. Supervisors can do this for every order. Each change and delete appears in **Team → Activity log**.
- **Send Invoice** sends the invoice PDF to the customer on WhatsApp.
- **Status templates**: messages sent to the customer automatically when you change the status (for example "Your order is on the way 🚚").

### How customers change or cancel

| Order status | What happens when the customer asks in chat |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | The bot changes or cancels it right away. |
| Confirmed / Processing | Your team gets a request and a notification – you decide. |
| Shipped | The chat goes to a person. |

You get a notification for every new order and every change request.

## Bookings

**Sales → Bookings** (for service businesses).

**Statuses**: **Requested** → **Confirmed** → **Done**, or **Cancelled**.

How the bot books:

1. The customer chooses a service and a date and time.
2. The bot checks your **opening hours** (from the AI sales bot settings – default 8:00–20:00), the service **duration** (default 60 minutes) and how many bookings you can take at the same time.
3. If the time is free, it saves a **Requested** booking and tells the customer. You confirm it in Bookings.
4. If the time is taken, it offers the **3 nearest free times**.

Settings on the Bookings page:

- **A time is taken when a booking is**: *Requested or Confirmed* (recommended) or *Confirmed only*.
- **Bookings at the same time**: 1–50 (for example 3 if you have 3 chairs in a salon).

Each booking can have notes and a **Send invoice** button. Customers can ask the bot to change or cancel a booking.
