# Billing, packages and AI credits

Open **Workspace → Billing & plan**.

## Packages

**Current package** shows your package, when it renews, and your credits this month. **Packages** lists what you can choose, with monthly or yearly prices (yearly is cheaper) and what each includes: AI credits per month, agents, products and services, channels (Messenger, Instagram), Social, CRM, marketing features and report history. A package **Made for you** appears when we have created a special package for your business.

Choosing a new package starts it right away. Paying again for the same package adds the time to your current end date.

## AI credits

- Every **AI bot reply** uses credits – about **1 credit per reply**. Replies by your team, flows, forms and broadcasts don't use AI credits.
- Your package gives a number of credits **every month**. Unused package credits don't carry over.
- At **80%** you get a warning. At **100%** the AI bot stops and chats go to your agents. Your inbox, agents, WhatsApp, orders and bookings keep working.
- The **Credits** card on the Dashboard and the banner on top show what is left.

### Top-ups (extra credits)

Buy a credit pack in Billing when you need more. Bought credits are valid for the number of days shown on the pack (for example 30 days) and are added to the credits you have left.

### Credits switch on more channels

On packages that include it, **active bought credits switch on the AI bot on WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram and Facebook / Instagram comment replies** – even if your package doesn't include Messenger or Instagram. When the bought credits are used up or expire, those channels close again.

## Free package

- The AI bot is **free for your first 30 days**. You see "Free AI bot until *date*".
- After that, the bot stops until you **buy credits** or choose a package. You see "Your free AI bot month has ended – buy credits to switch the bot back on."
- Everything else on Free keeps working: inbox, agents, WhatsApp, orders, contacts.

## Free trials

If you got a trial (for example Growth free for 1 month) you see "*Package* is free until *date* (N days left). Then you go back to Free." You get a reminder 3 days before the end. Choose a package any time to keep it – your data always stays.

## How to pay

### Card (PayHere)

Pay by Visa / Master card through PayHere. Tick **Renew automatically** to pay every month or year without doing anything. You can **Turn off** auto-renew any time in Billing.

### Bank transfer

1. Choose the package and **Pay by bank transfer**. You see our bank details.
2. Transfer the amount and **Upload slip** (photo or PDF).
3. We check the slip and activate your package – usually within a few hours on working days. You get a notification.
4. If something is wrong (for example a different amount), you get a notification with the reason and can upload again.

## Payments and invoices

**Payments & invoices** lists every payment with the amount, method, status and an **Invoice** PDF to download.

## When the package ends

If a package is not renewed, the AI bot stops and paid features close. Your data is kept. Pay or choose a package to continue. You get reminders 7 days and 1 day before the end (unless auto-renew is on).
