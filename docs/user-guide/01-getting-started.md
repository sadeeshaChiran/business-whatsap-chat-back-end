# Getting started

Welcome to {{brand}}. {{brand}} answers your customers on **WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger and Instagram** with an AI sales bot, takes orders and bookings, shares chats with your team, and shows which ads and posts bring you sales. This guide explains every part of the app, step by step.

## What you need

- A **Facebook account** that manages your business Facebook Page (for Messenger, Instagram, ads and WhatsApp Cloud API).
- A **WhatsApp number** for your business. It can be a new number or one you use in the WhatsApp Business app (Meta will move it to the Cloud API).
- Your **products or services** with prices – the bot only sells what you add.
- 20–30 minutes for the first setup.

## Create your account

1. Open the website and press **Start free**.
2. Enter your name, company name, email, **mobile number** and a password (at least 8 characters with letters and numbers).
3. Enter the two 6-digit codes: one we email you and one we send **by SMS** to your mobile. You do **not** need WhatsApp on that number.
4. You are in. Every new account starts on the **Free** package – nothing to pay.

> **Tip:** The Free package includes the AI bot **free for your first 30 days**. After that the bot needs AI credits (buy a top-up or choose a package). Your inbox, agents, WhatsApp, orders and everything else keep working on Free.

## The setup wizard

After your first login the **setup wizard** opens. It has six short steps and remembers where you stopped, so you can leave and come back.

| Step | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Business** | Your business name and what you sell: products, services, or both. This changes the menus (for example Bookings for services). |
| **Plan** | Shows your package (Free or a free trial). Nothing to pay now. |
| **Channel** | Connect WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram. While you connect, a **Back to setup** button floats on the screen. |
| **Catalog** | Add your products or services and your delivery fees. |
| **AI bot** | Tell the bot about your business: what you do, opening hours, payment methods. |
| **Ready** | A summary of what is done and what is left. |

You can open the wizard again any time from **Settings → Setup wizard** or **Help & guide**. The **Getting started** card on the Dashboard shows the same checklist until everything is done.

## Free trials

Sometimes we give a **free trial** of a paid package (for example Growth for one or two months). You see it in **Billing & plan** and in a banner: "*Package* is free until *date*". When the trial ends you go back to Free automatically – your data stays. Choose a package any time to keep the paid features.

## How the app is organised

The menu on the left is grouped:

| Group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Dashboard |
| Conversations | Inbox, Chat queue (agents see **My chats**) |
| CRM | CRM overview, Customer insights, Contacts, Pipeline, Tasks, Customer notes, Labels |
| Sales | Orders, Bookings, Products, Services, Delivery zones |
| AI & automation | AI sales bot, AI knowledge, Bot per customer, Bot reports, Automation flows, Forms |
| Marketing | Marketing overview, WhatsApp broadcasts, Message templates, Campaigns, Links & QR, Ads & leads, Meta ads, Audiences, Marketing settings |
| Social | Comments, Create post, Content calendar, Published posts, Social analytics, Saved replies, Page & Instagram |
| Team | Agents, Team board, Agent performance |
| Channels | WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram |
| Workspace | Settings, Billing & plan, Help & guide |

Some pages depend on your package. If a page is not in your package you see what it does and an **Upgrade** button.

**Quick dock** (computer): a floating bar at the bottom with the pages you use most – Home, Inbox, Orders, Products, Reports, Settings – and a **＋** for quick jobs (new order, add product, customers, reorder due …). It slides away while you scroll down and comes back when you scroll up; the small arrow tucks it away (press **Quick dock** to bring it back).

**Phone**: the bottom bar – **Home, Chats, ＋ (quick jobs), Orders, More** (the full menu). Add {{brand}} to your home screen to use it like an app.

**Search pages**: press **Ctrl K** (or the search button) and type – close it with **Esc**, the ✕ button or a tap outside.

## Owner and agents

- The **owner** (company admin) sees everything and changes settings.
- **Agents** are your staff. They only see the chats given to them (**My chats**), their customers' orders and notes. See [Team and agents](#team).

## The top bar

- **Notifications** (bell): new orders, chats that need a person, credits warnings, payment updates and announcements.
- **Light / Dark** (moon button).
- Agents also have the **Online / Offline** switch – only online agents get new chats.
